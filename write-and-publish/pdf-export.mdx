## Overview

PDF export creates a single PDF of your entire documentation site. Use it when you need a shareable offline copy, a reviewable snapshot, or a version you can distribute outside your live docs site.

When you request an export, Documentation.AI processes all pages in your site, combines them into one PDF, and sends you an email when the file is ready. You can also download the finished export from the dashboard.

## Plan availability

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">

PDF export is available on **Standard**, **Professional**, and **Enterprise** plans.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">

The **Starter** plan does not support PDF export.

</Callout>

## Request a PDF export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the documentation site you want to export" icon="monitor" title-type="p">

  Go to your documentation site in the dashboard. Make sure you are in the correct workspace if your organization manages more than one site.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new export" icon="download" title-type="p">

  Find the PDF export option for the site and request a new export.

  After you submit the request, the export starts processing in the background.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the completion email" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

  Documentation.AI emails you when the PDF is ready to download.

  Your export is complete when you receive the email notification and the download link appears in the dashboard.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">

  Open the download link from the email or return to the dashboard and download the completed export there.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you request an export

Your export runs in the background after you submit it. You do not need to keep the dashboard open while it processes.

When the export finishes, Documentation.AI sends an email notification to let you know the PDF is ready. The same download link is also available in the dashboard, so you can retrieve the file from either place.

## Limitations

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">

- **Exports expire after 7 days.** Download the PDF before the link expires.
- **Each export supports up to 8,000 pages.** Larger documentation sites cannot be exported as a single PDF.
- **Starting a new export cancels any export already in progress.** Request a new export only when you want to replace the current one.

</Callout>
