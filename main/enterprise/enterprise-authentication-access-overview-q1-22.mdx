## Login Methods

The top of the **Teammates** page controls how Teammates sign in. Choose the method that matches how your organization manages accounts.

**Method**

**How it works**

**Invitation**

Invite teammates directly by email. This is the quickest way to add someone and works as a standalone option, independent of Domain Safelist or SSO.

**Domain Safelist**

Set a domain so anyone with an email address at that domain can create a ReadMe account and join your group.

<strong>Single Sign-On (SAML)</strong>

Connect a SAML 2.0 identity provider so Teammates log in with your organization's credentials. Once configured, you can optionally **enforce SSO** to require every Teammate to log in through SAML before they can access your projects or group. See [Single Sign-On (SSO)](/main/enterprise/setting-up-sso).

## Invite Teammates

Inviting teammates directly is the quickest way to add people to your ReadMe Enterprise Group, and it works as a standalone option independent of a Domain Safelist or SSO. 

To invite a teammate, use the **Invite** button on the **Teammates** page in your group dashboard, then enter their email address and the role you want them to have.

## Manage Teammate Access

The lower portion of the **Teammates** page has a table displaying every teammate in the group. You can filter by every project in your Enterprise Group or by role. You can find, filter, and add Teammates, then adjust their access to some or all projects.

For a full breakdown of each role and how Group and project permissions combine, see [Teammates and Roles](/main/enterprise/user-roles).
