## Overview

If you're looking to find the ideal plan for your documentation needs, you're in the right spot. Let's dive into the options and features that make our plans as unique as your project. 🚀

### Payment Plans

#### Annual vs. Monthly Billing

Choose what suits you best: our more flexible monthly billing or the annual option that comes with a neat discount. It's all about giving you options that fit your project's timeline and budget.

#### ACH Payments

For those who prefer a streamlined approach to payments, we offer the option for ACH payments.

### Billing Security with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Security is a top priority, and we accommodate cards that require Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). This means that every transaction is double-checked and verified by the issuing bank, adding an extra layer of security to comply with local regulations.

## Exploring Our Plans

Discover the perfect [ReadMe plan](https://readme.com/pricing) tailored to your needs, whether you're just starting or require advanced features for your enterprise. Compare our diverse offerings, from Starter plans to the comprehensive Pro and Enterprise solutions, and find the best fit to enhance your documentation and developer experience.

### Starter Plan: Perfect for Growing Teams

The Starter Plan is ideal for growing teams looking to elevate their documentation.

- <strong>Price:</strong> $0/month
- <strong>Projects:</strong> 1 project included
- <strong>AI:</strong>

  - [AI Dropdown](https://docs.readme.com/main/docs/AskAI)
  - [LLMs.txt](https://docs.readme.com/main/docs/LLMstxt)
  - [MCP Server](/main/docs/mcp)
  - [Ask AI](/main/docs/ask-ai) ($150 / month)
- <strong>Features:</strong>

  - Bidirectional Sync
  - Interactive API Reference
  - Custom Domains
  - Usage Metrics
  - Markdown Editor
  - Customizable Themes

### Pro Plan: Advanced Features for Scaling Businesses

The Pro Plan offers advanced features for businesses needing more robust documentation solutions.

- <strong>Price:</strong> $250/month
- <strong>Projects:</strong> 1 project included
- <strong>AI:</strong>

  - All Starter AI features
  - [Ask AI Lite](/main/docs/ask-ai)
  - [AI Linting](/main/docs/linter)
  - [Docs Audit Preview](/main/docs/docs-audit)
  - [AI Writer](/main/docs/github-ai-writer)
- <strong>Features:</strong>

  - Everything in the Starter Plan
  - Reviews
  - Branching in ReadMe
  - Custom HTML & CSS
  - Reusable content
  - Export Metrics
  - Custom Integrations

### Enterprise Plan: Customized Solutions for Large Organizations

The Enterprise Plan provides customized solutions for large organizations with complex documentation needs.

- <strong>Price:</strong> Custom pricing
- <strong>Projects:</strong> Custom (multiple combined projects available)
- <strong>AI:</strong>

  - All AI features in the Pro plan
  - Full docs audit with history
  - Ask AI API access
  - Private AI context
  - Global lint rules
- <strong>Features:</strong>

  - Everything in the Pro Plan
  - Dedicated account manager
  - Priority support
  - Custom SLAs
  - Enterprise-grade security features
  - Custom onboarding and training

If you're considering the Enterprise Plan, you can [sign up for a demo](https://readme.com/enterprise) or reach out to [growth@readme.io](mailto:growth@readme.io).

### 14-day Free Trial

Start your journey with a 14-day trial that offers all the features of our Pro Plan. This is your opportunity to test out our features before you decide on your long-term plan.

- <strong>Duration:</strong> 14 days of full access to all Pro plan features
- <strong>Features:</strong> Take advantage of the Pro Plan, including custom CSS/HTML, reusable content and more!
- <strong>Transition:</strong> When you’re ready to publish your docs, you can upgrade to any of our paid plans or move to our Starter plan. Note that if you downgrade to Starter, you'll lose some features, including reusable content. Clear options, no surprises.
- <strong>No Credit Card Required:</strong> Start your trial without needing to provide credit card details.
