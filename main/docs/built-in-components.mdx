ReadMe provides several powerful MDX components out of the box and accessible directly from the slash menu. For community-built components, check out our [Marketplace](/main/docs/building-custom-mdx-components?isFramePreview=true#marketplace) in the **Editor > Custom Components** page.

### Tabs

Organize related content into easily navigable sections.

**Tabs Example**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First Tab">
    Welcome to the content that you can only see inside the first Tab.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Second Tab">
    Here's content that's only inside the second Tab.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Third Tab">
    Here's content that's only inside the third Tab.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

### Accordion

Present information in collapsible sections.

**Accordion Example**

<Expandable title="My Accordion Title">
  Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, <strong>consectetur adipiscing elit.</strong> Ut enim
  ad minim veniam, quis nostrud exercitation ullamco. Excepteur sint
  occaecat cupidatat non proident!
</Expandable>

---

### Cards

Display content in a clean, grid-like format.

**Cards Example**

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Card One" icon="rocket">
    Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Card Two" icon="code">
    Consectetur adipiscing elit.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Card Three" icon="comments">
    Ut enim ad minim veniam.
  </Card>
</Columns>

---

### Columns

Creates a multi-column layout where content is displayed side-by-side rather than stacked vertically.

<Columns cols={2}>
  Neque porro quisquam est qui dolorem ipsum quia

  *Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit*

  > Ut enim ad minim veniam, quis nostrud ullamco
</Columns>
