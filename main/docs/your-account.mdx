## Account Settings

Access your Account Settings directly from the main project dashboard, or open any project and navigate to **Admin Settings** → **User icon** → **Account Settings**.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793774587535-xqyl8so4mz8-de1bfd6146a3f3e7dcdce07ae88603616122c7bbabecb9b760a88466b0f33dfb-236dff7a9271cd05afe8fd5cab86187f.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

In Account Settings, you can update your personal preferences and profile details. These changes apply automatically to every project you’re part of.

## Cancel Your Subscription

In the legacy dashboard, scroll to the bottom of **Settings > General**.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793774589081-7slea4lba38-a3364e5-delete-project.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Cancel Subscription to stop paying and revert your project to a trial." caption="Cancel Subscription to stop paying and revert your project to a trial." />

Only the [Project Owner](/main/docs/manage-team) has permission to do this. Project owners are also able to delete the project and all associated data.

<Callout>
  ### Make Sure You're 100% Sure Before You Delete a Project!

  <strong>Warning:</strong> Deleting a project is irreversible, even by ReadMe Support, so be extra sure before deleting!
</Callout>
