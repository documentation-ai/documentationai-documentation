## FAQ

<Expandable title="Are AI features enabled by default?">
  No. You control which features are enabled. Ask AI, MCP, and LLMs.txt are toggled on or off in AI Settings. Agent, Linter, and Docs Audit do not process your documentation unless you actively use them.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What happens to my data?">
  AI features are powered by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. In all cases, your data is sent only when you actively use the feature. No AI provider uses your data for model training.
</Expandable>
