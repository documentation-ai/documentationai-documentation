## Request a Review

1. Mark your branch as ready for review, then open the review tab and select <strong>Add +</strong>.
2. Choose the teammates whose approval you need. You can tag only teammates who have approval access under your project settings.
3. Confirm your selection to send each tagged reviewer a notification.

## Notifications

ReadMe sends each tagged reviewer a notification that their review was requested. Select **Review** in the notification to open the branch, review the proposed changes, and approve them.

## Track Approvals

The approval section of the review tab lists tagged reviewers and reviewers who were not tagged. A tagged reviewer's yellow status indicator turns green after they approve the branch.

Use these indicators to identify which requested approvals are complete and which reviews are still pending.

## Companion Features

Use the **Reviews Dashboard** (the page that lists branches awaiting review) to see tagged reviewers for branches marked **Need Review**. The dashboard shows tagged reviewers only when a branch has at least one tagged reviewer.

Filter the Reviews Dashboard to show only branches where you are a tagged reviewer.
