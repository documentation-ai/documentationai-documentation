---
title: 'Add Excluded Dates'
description: >-
  Block specific dates for every trip in WP Travel Engine 5.x when your business
  is closed or unavailable.
---
<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This page is for WP Travel Engine 5.x. Version 6 added block behaviors, time slots, per-trip exclusions and yearly repeats.
</Callout>

Excluded dates block specific days for every trip on your site. Travelers cannot book a departure that starts on an excluded date.

**How to set excluded dates:**

1. Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Extensions > Fixed Starting Dates**.
2. Scroll to the **Excluded Dates** field.
3. Click the field and pick a date from the calendar. Repeat to add more dates, one at a time.
4. Click **Save Settings**.

To remove a date, click the **×** next to it in the Excluded Dates field, then save.

**What version 5 does not support:**

- Blocking a range of dates in one step. Add each date separately.
- Choosing a block behavior. An excluded date always blocks departures that start on that day, while trips already in progress continue.
- Blocking individual time slots.
- Applying an exclusion to specific trips. Every excluded date applies to all trips.
- Repeating an exclusion every year. Add the date again for each year.

**Examples of using excluded dates:**

- Blocking public holidays when your office is closed
- Excluding days when guides are unavailable
- Marking company retreat days when no departures should start

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **Important Note**: Excluded dates in version 5 apply to all trips. If a single trip must stay bookable on a closed day, remove the date from the list.
</Callout>
