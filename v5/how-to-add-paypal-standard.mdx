## Add PayPal Standard to your checkout

PayPal Standard lets travelers pay for trip bookings through PayPal. In version 5, you configure and enable the gateway from one screen.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Before you start, make sure WP Travel Engine is active and you have the email address of your PayPal business account ready.
</Callout>

## Set up PayPal Standard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PayPal Standard settings" title-type="p">
    From the left sidebar of your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payment > PayPal Standard**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the gateway" title-type="p">
    Check **Enable PayPal Standard** at the top of the screen. This is the only switch needed to show PayPal at checkout. Version 5 has no separate General payments screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your PayPal email" title-type="p">
    Enter the email address of your PayPal business account in **PayPal Email**. Booking payments are sent to this account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test with sandbox mode" title-type="p">
    To test bookings without real money, check **Enable Sandbox Mode** and use a PayPal sandbox account. Uncheck it before you go live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes" title-type="p">
    Click **Save Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported currencies

PayPal Standard in WP Travel Engine 5.x accepts payments in these currencies:

- USD (United States Dollar)
- EUR (Euro)
- GBP (British Pound)
- AUD (Australian Dollar)

Your base currency must be one of these, or PayPal rejects the payment.
