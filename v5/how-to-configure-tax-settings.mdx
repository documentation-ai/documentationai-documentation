<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This page is for WP Travel Engine 5.x. Version 6 moved tax to the Payments settings and added the option to include tax in the trip price.
</Callout>

## Overview

WP Travel Engine 5.x can add a tax percentage to every booking. Tax is always added at checkout as a separate line. Version 5 cannot show trip prices with tax already included.

## How to enable tax

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the tax settings" title-type="p">
    In the left sidebar of your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine** > **Settings** > **Extensions** > **Tax**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable tax" title-type="p">
    Check the **Enable Tax** checkbox.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the tax percentage" title-type="p">
    Enter the percentage in **Tax Percentage**. The same rate applies to every trip on your site. Version 5 has no per-trip tax rate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" title-type="p">
    Click **Save Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tax label

The **Tax Label** field sets the text shown next to the tax amount at checkout. The default label is `Tax`.

The label is plain text. Version 5 does not support the `%s` and `%%` placeholders, so the rate is not inserted into the label automatically. To show the rate, type it into the label yourself, for example `VAT 13%`.

## How tax appears at checkout

Say you run a day tour for $100 and the tax percentage is 10. The traveler sees $100 on the trip page. At checkout, they see $100 plus $10 tax for a total of $110.
