</Callout>

The Partial Payment add-on lets travelers book a trip by paying part of the total cost upfront.

## Key features

- Travelers pay a percentage of the trip price as a deposit.
- One deposit percentage applies to every trip on your site.
- Optionally let travelers choose between the deposit and the full price.

## Prerequisites

- WordPress version 5.2 or higher.
- WP Travel Engine 5.x installed and activated.
- Partial Payment add-on installed and activated.

Version 5 does not require WP Travel Engine Pro for this add-on.

## Feature configuration

### Enabling Partial Payment

Go to **WP Travel Engine → Settings → Extensions → Partial Payment**.

- Check **Enable Partial Payment**.
- Enter the deposit in **Partial Payment Percentage**. The default is 20%.
- Check **Allow Full Payment** if travelers may also pay the full price at checkout.
- Click **Save Settings**.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  **Example:** You set the partial payment to 25%. A traveler books a trip that costs $800.

  Initial deposit = 25% of $800 = **$200**
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Version 5 supports percentage deposits only. Fixed amounts per traveler or per booking are not available.
</Callout>

### Collecting the remaining balance

Version 5 does not send automatic reminders for the remaining balance. To collect it, open the booking under **Bookings → All Bookings**, check the **Due** amount, and contact the traveler yourself.

There are no cut-off days in version 5. Travelers can pay the deposit for any departure date, including last-minute bookings.

### Trip-specific settings

Version 5 has no per-trip partial payment settings. The global percentage applies to every trip, and partial payment cannot be turned off for a single trip.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I set different partial payment amounts for different trips?" default-open="false">
    No. In version 5, one percentage applies to every trip.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How are customers reminded to pay the remaining amount?" default-open="false">
    They are not reminded automatically in version 5. You follow up with each traveler from the booking details.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
