## Documentation your users actually read

Your users are humans and their AI agents — GitBook publishes for both. Write in the editor, drive it from your agent, or sync from Git.

[Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) [GitBook MCP](/docs/docs-as-code/gitbook-mcp)

### AI out of the box

Two work for you while you write. Three work for your readers after you publish.

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="GitBook Agent" href="/docs/gitbook-agent/overview" horizontal="false">
    WRITES WITH YOU

    Drafts, edits, reviews change requests, and flags gaps against your style guide.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI Assistant" href="/docs/ai-for-your-readers/gitbook-ai-assistant" horizontal="false">
    ANSWERS READERS

    Answers questions on your site from your docs and connected support sources, in the sidebar or search box.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI Insights" href="/docs/analytics/insights/ai-insights" horizontal="false">
    IMPROVES DOCS

    See what your visitors are asking and identify knowledge gaps in your docs.
