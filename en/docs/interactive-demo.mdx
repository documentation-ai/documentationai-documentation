Document360's built-in interactive video lets readers engage with a product simulation directly inside your article - clicking and exploring a guided interface instead of watching a video or reading instructions. Everything is captured and edited within the **Editor**.

---

## What you can do with an interactive video

- **Record** your screen, a Chrome tab, or a specific window with optional audio
- **Edit** the demo with hotspots, blur, spotlight, text, image, camera overlay, highlight, crop, trim, and music
- **Insert** the final demo directly into your article for readers to interact with

---

## When to use an interactive video

Technical writers often struggle to convey complex workflows through text alone. Screenshots fall short when the process involves multiple clicks across different screens. An interactive video lets writers record the exact workflow, add hotspots, blur sensitive data, and guide readers through each step in a hands-on environment, all without leaving Document360.
