The **Voice** feature lets you control how audio is delivered in your explainer video or interactive video. You can keep the original audio, replace it with an AI-generated voiceover, or remove narration entirely. You can also add and manage subtitles from this section.

---

## Voice options

In the **Voice** section, choose one of the following options:

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Original voice** | Uses the original background audio from the explainer video or interactive video |
| **AI voice** | Replaces the narration with an AI-generated voiceover |
| **No voice** | Removes all narration from the explainer video or interactive video |

---

## Set up AI voiceover

1. Select **AI voice** in the Voice section.
2. Click **AI voiceover**.
3. In the **Voice over** panel, select a voice from the dropdown. Voices are available in multiple languages.
4. Under the **Script** section:

   - If the demo already has subtitles, the voiceover is auto-generated from them. The generated script appears in the Script section, and you can edit it as needed.
   - If there are no subtitles, click **Add now** to manually enter your narration script.
5. The script is rendered and used to generate the AI voiceover.

---

## Add subtitles

1. Click **Subtitles** in the Voice section.
2. If the demo has a voiceover, auto-generated subtitles appear here. Edit them as needed.
3. If there is no voiceover, manually enter your subtitle script.
4. Enable the **Display subtitles** checkbox to show subtitles in the published demo.

 NOTE

Subtitle editing is disabled when AI narration is turned on. Turn off AI narration to make changes to subtitles.

---

## Voiceover and subtitles in other languages

If you translate the explainer video or interactive video into other languages, each language version has its own voiceover script and subtitles.

- **AI voiceover** \- When you translate the video or demo, the script is translated and a new voiceover is generated for that language. The voice changes to match the new language automatically.
- **Subtitles** \- Subtitles are translated with the rest of the content. To adjust them, open that language version and edit the subtitles as usual.

To create the language versions, translate the video or demo from [Document360 Engage](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/document360-engage#translate-content).

Readers hear the voiceover and see the subtitles for the language they select on the knowledge base site. If the video or demo isn't translated into that language, readers get the default language version.

---

## Save changes

Click **Close** to save all changes made in the Voice panel.

---

## Best practices

- <strong>Use AI voice for consistency.</strong> AI voiceovers maintain the same tone and pace throughout the demo, unlike recorded audio which may vary.
- <strong>Always review the script before applying.</strong> Small errors in the script will carry through to the voiceover.
- <strong>Enable subtitles for accessibility.</strong> Subtitles help viewers follow along in noisy environments or when audio is unavailable.
- <strong>Edit auto-generated subtitles.</strong> Auto-generated subtitles may not always be perfectly accurate. Review and correct them before publishing.
- <strong>Turn off AI narration before editing subtitles.</strong> Subtitle editing is disabled while AI narration is active.
- <strong>Review each language version.</strong> After translating, listen to the voiceover and read the subtitles in each language to check pronunciation, pacing, and terminology.
