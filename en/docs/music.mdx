---
title: Music
---

The Music feature lets you add ambient background music to your video, helping set the tone and improve the overall viewing experience.

 Note

Background music plays only after publishing. It will not play in edit mode.

---

## Add background music to explainer video or interactive video

1. In the right pane, go to **Background Music**.
2. Enable the **Enable background music** toggle.
3. Click **Choose from Gallery** to open the audio picker.
4. Select a preset track from the available options (e.g., Acoustic, Happy, Upbeat). Supported formats: `.mp3`, `.aac`
5. Click **Insert** to add the selected track.
6. Use the **Volume** slider to adjust the audio level.
7. Enable **Fade In Fade Out** for a smoother listening experience.
8. Enable **Pause on Interaction** to pause the music when the viewer interacts with the video.
9. Click **Apply**.

## Managing background music

- To change the track, click **Replace audio** and select a different one.
- To remove background music, disable the **Enable background music** toggle.
