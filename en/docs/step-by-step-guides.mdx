Document360's **Step by step guide** feature turns any workflow into a visual, step-by-step walkthrough.

A step by step guide is a structured set of instructions designed to lead users through a specific process or task in a sequential manner, breaking it down into easy-to-follow steps.

---

## When to use step by step guides

Step by step guides work best when the process is visual, sequential, and hard to follow from text alone. Common use cases include:

- **Product onboarding** \- Walk new users through account setup, first-time configuration, or a core workflow so they reach value faster without contacting support.
- **Software tutorials** \- Document a UI-heavy task like configuring an integration or setting up a report, where readers benefit from seeing each screen in sequence.
- **Internal SOPs** \- Capture internal processes (e.g., how to submit a procurement request or run a deployment checklist) so institutional knowledge is preserved and repeatable.
- **Feature announcements** \- Accompany a product release note with a guided walkthrough of the new feature, reducing the volume of "how do I use this?" tickets.
- **Troubleshooting flows** \- Guide users through a diagnostic sequence (e.g., checking a setting, clearing a cache, re-authenticating) where the exact screen state matters.

---

## Step by step guides and AI discoverability

Step by step guides are built for readers in a browser, but they are also structured for AI agents and LLMs to read. When you publish a guide in a public workspace, Document360 makes it available in the same machine-readable form as the rest of your knowledge base:

- The guide is published as a Markdown (`.md`) page, so AI crawlers receive the step sequence without navigation elements or page chrome.
- You can set an AI & search description (GEO/SEO) for the guide, or generate one with Ask Eddy AI.
- The guide is listed in your knowledge base's [llms.txt](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/llms-txt) file alongside your articles and page categories.

Procedural questions are among the most common things readers ask an AI assistant. A guide with a clear title and a well-written description is one that an assistant can retrieve and cite by name.

To set the title and description for a guide, read the article on [Editing a Step by step guide](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/editing-a-step-by-step-guide).
