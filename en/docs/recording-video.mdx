Document360's built-in screen recorder lets you record your screen directly from the **Editor** or from **Document360 Engage**. You can record your screen alone or alongside your camera, and insert the video straight into your article.

---

## Before you begin

The screen recorder requires the Document360 AI Capture extension. The extension only needs to be installed once and is supported on Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Brave.

---

## Setting up the recorder

To set up the screen recorder,

1. Navigate to the desired article in the Advanced WYSIWYG editor.
2. Type the **Slash** command `/Explainer video`, then select **Explainer video** > **Create new video**.<br /> To insert an explainer video you have already recorded, select **Choose existing video** instead.
3. If you haven't installed the **Document360 AI Capture** extension, click **Install**.<br /> Follow the on-screen steps in your browser to complete the installation.

 NOTE

For browser-specific installation steps, see [Installing the Document360 AI Capture extension](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/install-document360-ai-capture-extension).

4. Once installed, return to the Knowledge base portal.

## Recording from Document360 Engage

You can also record a video without opening an article first.

1. Click **Document360 Engage** () in the left navigation bar.
2. Under **Create flows**, click **Explainer video**.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793777560932-d74jg0uj58b-explainer-video-from-engage.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Document360 Engage page with Explainer video selected under Create flows" />

3. Follow the steps in [Recording the screen](/en/docs/recording-video#recording-the-screen).

   The video isn't added to any article yet. It appears in the Document360 Engage list, and you can insert it into any article when you need it. For more information, see [Document360 Engage](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/document360-engage).

## Recording the screen

When you create a new explainer video, the **Create explainer video** dialog opens.

1. In the **Title** field, enter a name for the video.
2. In the **Language** dropdown, select the language you want to record in. It's set to your project's default language, and you can select any language you have access to.
3. Click **Create**.
4. The **Video capture** panel will appear. Select one of the following recording types and click **Continue**:

   - **Screen only video**: Captures only the screen.

     1. If you select **Screen only video**, the **Screen only video** panel will appear.
     2. Select the desired audio from the dropdown, if required. If no audio is needed, select **No audio**.
     3. Click **Next**.
   - **Screen + Camera video**: Captures both the screen and the presenter via the camera.

     1. If you select **Screen + Camera video**, the **Screen + Camera video** panel will appear.
     2. Select the desired **camera** and **audio** options from the dropdown menus.
     3. Click **Next**.
5. Select the screen to capture:

   - **Browser tab**: Records only the selected browser tab.
   - **Window**: Records only the selected window.
6. Click **Share**. The selected screen will appear along with the **Screen recording** panel.
7. Choose the recommended resolution (16:9, 4:3, or 1:1).
8. Optionally, before starting:

   - Click **Add** in the **Blur selected** section to hide sensitive areas.
   - Enable **Show recording controls on screen** if needed. A Document360 logo appears at the bottom left; clicking it pauses the recording. This logo will also be visible in the explainer video.
9. Click **Start capturing** to begin recording. A recording can be up to 5 minutes long.
10. Click **Discard** to cancel and return to the previous screen.

During recording, mouse clicks and interactions will be captured, with **click indicators** displayed for easier video editing.

11. Click **Pause** to pause the recording and **Record** to resume.
12. To stop recording, click **Stop sharing** in the floating panel or **Stop recording** in the **Screen recording** panel.

After recording, the video takes a few moments to process. Once ready, it opens in the video editor and appears in Document360 Engage. If you started from an article, the video is also inserted into the article.

## Best practices

- <strong>Plan before recording:</strong> Outline the steps you want to capture to avoid unnecessary retakes.
- **Use blur before recording** if you know sensitive content will appear. It's easier than adding it in the editor later.
- **Choose 16:9** resolution for articles viewed primarily on desktop.
- **Record in a quiet environment** if using the original voice option.
- <strong>Keep recordings focused:</strong> Shorter, task-specific videos are easier for readers to follow than long walkthroughs.
