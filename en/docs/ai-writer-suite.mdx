The AI writer suite in Document360 is a powerful collection of AI-driven tools designed to streamline your content creation process, enhance the quality of your articles, and ensure your documentation remains engaging and relevant. Available directly within the knowledge base portal, this suite offers features that assist with everything from writing assistance and SEO optimization to generating FAQs, recommending related content, and summarizing changes between article versions, all aimed at boosting productivity and improving the reader experience.

---

## Features in the AI writer suite

1. <strong>[AI writing agent](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-writing-assistant)</strong>

   1. **Create article using prompt**<br /> Generate a full article from a text prompt.
   2. **Create article from video**<br /> Create an article from an uploaded video, with optional screenshots.
   3. **Create article from audio, transcript, or text file**<br /> Convert uploaded files into a structured article.
   4. **Outline creation**<br /> Generate a structured outline using a prompt, video, or file.
   5. **Continue writing**<br /> Extend existing article content using your draft.
   6. **Rewrite article**<br /> Regenerate the entire article using a refined prompt.
   7. **Generate FAQ**<br /> Create FAQs based on the generated/existing article.
   8. **Generate summary**<br /> Produce a concise summary of the article.
2. <strong>[AI writer](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-writer)</strong>

   1. **Make longer/Make shorter**<br /> Adjust the length of your content with ease, either by expanding or condensing content based on your needs.
   2. **Change tone/Change voice**<br /> Modify the tone or voice of your content to match your audience or brand guidelines.
   3. **Split sentences**<br /> Break up long, complex sentences into shorter ones to improve readability.
   4. **Convert speech**<br /> Switch between active and passive voice based on your documentation needs.
   5. **Improve it**<br /> Enhance your writing by making it clearer, more concise, or more impactful.
   6. **Convert into table**<br /> Automatically convert a list or paragraph into a neatly formatted table for easier data presentation.
3. <strong>[AI FAQ generator](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-faq-generator)</strong><br /> Automatically create frequently asked questions (FAQs) based on the content of your article.
4. <strong>[AI title recommender](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-title-recommender)</strong><br /> Receive AI-driven title suggestions to make your articles more engaging and easier to find.
5. <strong>[AI SEO description generator](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/seo-description-generator)</strong><br /> Automatically generate search engine-friendly descriptions to boost the visibility of your articles in the search engine results.
6. <strong>[AI tag recommender](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-tag-recommender)</strong><br /> Get relevant tag suggestions to help categorize and improve the discoverability of your content within the knowledge base.
7. <strong>[AI related articles recommender](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-related-articles-recommender)</strong><br /> Suggest relevant articles to users based on the current content, helping them find additional information quickly.
8. <strong>[AI Chart generator](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-chart-generator)</strong><br /> Generate pie charts, flow charts, and tables using Eddy AI based on the selected content.
9. <strong>[AI alt text generator](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-alt-text-generator)</strong><br /> Generate alt text for images and videos while uploading using Eddy AI to enhance user experience, improve accessibility, and boost search rankings.
10. <strong>[Summarize changes](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/revision-history#summarize-changes-with-eddy-ai)</strong><br /> Get a plain-language summary of what was added, removed, or rewritten between two versions of an article, so you can review drafts faster in revision history.

## Multilingual support for Eddy AI writer suite

The Eddy AI writer suite seamlessly integrates with the Knowledge base site, offering support for a wider array of languages.

In addition to English (en, en-US, en-GB, en-AU), Eddy AI writer suite supports 21 other languages, including French (fr), Spanish (es), German (de), Norwegian (no), Dutch (nl), Portuguese (pt), Swedish (sv), Italian (it), Chinese (zh, zh-hant, zh-cn, zh-tw), Korean (ko), Finnish (fi), Polish (pl), Arabic (ar), Hebrew (he), Danish (da), Brazilian Portuguese (pt-br), Japanese (ja), Czech (cs), Lithuanian (lt), Spanish (Mexico) (es-mx), Greek (el), and Cantonese (yue).

If a workspace is configured with a language not supported by Eddy AI, the writer suite features will not be available for that workspace.

 NOTE

The **AI writing agent** within the writer suite is available in all languages supported by Eddy AI.

## Usage limits

Here's a quick overview of the key credit soft limits per month:

| Feature | Credit limit |
| --- | --- |
| Make longer | 5000 credits |
| Make shorter | 5000 credits |
| Change tone | 5000 credits |
| AI SEO description generator | 1000 credits |
| AI Title recommender | 1000 credits |
| Summarize changes | 1000 credits |

A total of 5000 credits is allocated per month for the AI writer suite features. You can distribute these credits across any of the listed features based on your needs.

For example, you can use all 5000 credits for Make longer, or split them across multiple features, such as 4000 for Make longer and 1000 for AI Title recommender. However, **AI SEO description generator** and **AI Title recommender** each have a soft limit of 1000 credits per month.

Credits are refreshed automatically at the beginning of every month and do not carry over. If you're approaching your limit, you will be notified within the platform.

These limits do not apply to the AI writing agent, which has its own separate credit allocation system.

### Summarize changes credit limits

**Summarize changes** has a monthly limit of 1,000 credits.

- Each time Eddy AI generates a summary, 1 credit is used, regardless of the length of the article or the summary.
- Opening a saved summary for the same pair of versions doesn't use a credit, whether you or a teammate opens it.
- Regenerating a summary after the content changes uses 1 credit.

Credits reset at the beginning of every month, and unused credits do not roll over. For more information, see [Summarize changes with Eddy AI](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/revision-history#summarize-changes-with-eddy-ai).

### AI writing agent credit limits

The AI writing agent is part of the AI writer suite but uses a separate set of credits. **Its credit limits and usage are independent of the other AI writer suite features**.

Credits are reset at the beginning of every month, and unused credits will not roll over to the next cycle.

Each AI writing agent operation consumes a certain number of credits depending on the type of action performed.

| Operation | Credits deducted per article generation |
| --- | --- |
| Write an article using MP3 (30 mins) | 10 |
| Write an article using MP4 (10 mins) with up to 40 input images | 10 |
| Write an article using Transcript (TXT/DOCX) | 5 |
| Continue writing, Outline generation, Rewrite, and Refine | 1 |
| Write an article using prompt | 1 |

If you reach your monthly limit, you can contact the [Document360 Support team](https://document360.com/support/).
