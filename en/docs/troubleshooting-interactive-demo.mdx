  ### FAQ

  <Expandable title="Why does the interactive video capture window close immediately after clicking 'Start Capturing'?">
    This can occur if the target application has screen capture restrictions or security protections enabled at the OS or application level. This is the same restriction that affects Step by step guide recording, see [Troubleshooting step by step guides for the full explanation and resolution steps](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/troubleshooting-advanced-content-creation-tools).
