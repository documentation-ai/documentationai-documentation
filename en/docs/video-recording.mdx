Document360's built-in AI video capturing lets you record, edit, and publish screen recordings directly within your knowledge base. From capturing your screen to adding interactive elements and voiceovers, everything happens inside the **Editor**.

---

## What you can do with an explainer video

- **Record** your screen, a specific window, or a Chrome tab
- **Edit** the recording with blur, spotlight, crop, trim, and voiceover
- **Insert** the final video directly into your article

---

## When to use an explainer video

Technical writers often juggle multiple tools just to add a video to an article, recording in one app, editing in another, then uploading the result.

Document360 eliminates that by bringing screen recording and editing into the editor itself.

Writers can capture a workflow, blur sensitive data, trim dead time, and add a voiceover, all within the editor.
