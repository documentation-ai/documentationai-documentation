## Before you begin

Step by step guides are created and recorded using the **Document360 AI Capture** extension. You'll be prompted to install it during setup. It's a one-time step.

---

## Create a Step by step guide

Document360 offers multiple ways to create Step by step guides, depending on where you are in the portal.

The most common way to create a Step by step guide is from the **Create** dropdown on the top bar:

1. Navigate to **Documentation** () in the Knowledge base portal.
2. Click the **Create** dropdown on the top bar and select **Step by step guide**.

### Other ways to create a Step by step guide

All paths lead to the same **Create Step by step guide** dialog. You can also reach it from:

- <strong>Document360 Engage:</strong> click **Document360 Engage** () in the left navigation bar > under **Create flows**, click **Step by step guide**
- <strong>Categories & Articles pane:</strong> click the More () icon next to a category > **Create Step by step guide**
- <strong>Between two articles or categories:</strong> hover between two articles or categories in the **Categories & Articles** pane > click the **Flywheel** () icon > **Step by step guide**
- <strong>Page category editor:</strong> click the More () icon next to the **Publish** button > **Create Step by step guide**
- <strong>Empty index category:</strong> click the More () icon on an index type category > **Step by step guide**

---

## Name and set up the Step by step guide

After selecting Step by step guide through any of the above ways, the **Create Step by step guide** dialog will appear.

1. In the **Title** field, enter your desired guide name with a maximum of 300 characters. Special characters and reserved words are not allowed.
2. If you started from Document360 Engage, select the workspace for the guide in the **Workspace** dropdown.
3. Choose a desired category in the **Category** dropdown. Only the categories you have access to are listed.
4. Click **Create**.

Your guide also appears in [Document360 Engage](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/document360-engage), where you can translate it, clone it, and see the articles it's linked to.

## Install the extension

 NOTE

Some desktop applications have screen capture restrictions or security protections enabled at the OS or application level. These applications cannot be recorded by Document360 AI Capture or any other screen recording tool, and capturing will fail or cancel immediately after starting. If you're unsure whether an application you plan to record is affected, check with your application administrator before starting a recording session.

If this is your first time creating a step by step guide, you'll see a prompt to install the **Document360 AI Capture** extension.

1. Click **Install** when prompted.
2. Follow the on-screen steps in your browser to complete the installation.

 NOTE

For browser-specific installation steps, see [Installing the Document360 AI Capture extension](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/install-document360-ai-capture-extension).

3. Once installed, return to the Knowledge base portal and click **Start capturing**.
4. Select the desired screen to record:

   1. **Chrome tab**: Records only the selected Chrome tab.
   2. **Window**: Records only the selected window.

 NOTE

- Choose Chrome tab when the entire workflow lives within the browser, such as demonstrating a web app or walking through a portal.
- Choose Window when the workflow involves a desktop application or requires switching between a browser and another tool on your screen.

## Record your steps

Once the extension is installed and the desired screen is selected,

1. Click **Share**.<br /> The Step by step guide recording panel will appear.
2. Configure the following in the recording panel:

   1. Choose your preferred **resolution**, either 16:9 or 4:3.
   2. The **Total clicks** section tracks the clicks made during the recording.
   3. Click **Add** in the **Blur selected** section to mask any sensitive information on screen before recording begins.
   4. Turn on the **Show recording controls on the screen** toggle if needed.
   5. Click **Discard** to cancel and return to the previous screen.
3. Click **Start capturing** to begin recording.<br /> Once recording begins, navigate through the screens and perform the necessary actions. All on-screen interactions, including mouse clicks and typing, will be recorded. A recording status icon will appear to show that the recording is active.

 NOTE

- If the **Show recording controls on the screen** toggle is turned on, a Document360 logo will appear in the bottom left corner of the recording screen. Clicking the logo will pause the recording. This logo will also be visible in screenshots.
- Mouse clicks and interactions will be recorded, with click indicators displayed for easier guide editing.

---

## Pause and resume recording

1. To pause at any point, switch back to the Document360 tab.
2. In the recording panel, click **Pause capturing**.
3. When you're ready to continue, click **Resume**.
4. To stop the recording entirely, click **Done** in the recording panel or **Stop sharing** in the floating panel.<br /> After recording, the steps are automatically uploaded to the guide editor in the Knowledge base portal. Each step will display the recorded screens with content.

## Best practices

A little preparation before you start recording saves significant editing time afterward.

- <strong>Close unnecessary tabs and notifications:</strong> Browser notifications, email pop-ups, and unrelated tabs can appear in the recording. Silence them before you start.
- <strong>Use a clean browser profile:</strong> Bookmarks bars, extensions, and saved form data can appear on screen. Consider recording in a clean browser profile or private/incognito window.
- <strong>Pre-blur sensitive areas:</strong> If your workflow involves credentials, customer data, or internal URLs, use the **Blur selected** section before recording rather than blurring frame-by-frame in the editor.
- <strong>Go slow and deliberate:</strong> Hover briefly over each element before clicking. This gives readers time to orient themselves and produces cleaner click indicators in the recorded steps.
- <strong>Stick to one task per guide:</strong> A guide covering "set up your account AND configure your first report" will be hard to navigate. Record separate guides for each distinct task and link them.
- <strong>Name the guide after the task, not the screen.</strong> "Configure a SAML 2.0 identity provider" is retrievable by both readers and AI agents. "SSO settings" is not.
- <strong>Add an AI & search description before you publish.</strong> Step by step guides are structured for AI agents and LLMs to read and are listed in your [llms.txt](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/llms-txt) file. A guide published without a description is listed by title only. See [Editing a Step by step guide](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/editing-a-step-by-step-guide).

## FAQ

<Expandable title="Does Eddy AI use content from Step by step guides?">
  Yes. Eddy AI fetches the title, description, and body text from published guides when responding to searches. Responses include a citation linking directly to the source guide.

  External AI agents are served separately. A published guide is available as a Markdown (`.md`) page and is listed in your knowledge base's [llms.txt](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/llms-txt) file, so assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can retrieve and cite it too.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I schedule a guide for later publication?">
  Click the dropdown arrow next to **Publish** > **Publish later** > set the date, time, and time zone > optionally add a comment > click **Schedule**.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why is a guide locked, and how do I unlock it?">
  A guide locks when someone is editing it. It unlocks automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity, when the editor clicks the Lock icon and selects Unlock, or when they navigate away.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How many steps can I create in a guide?">
  Up to 200 steps per guide.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I track activity events for step by step guides?">
  To monitor activity events in the Step by step guide:

  1. Navigate to **Settings** () > **Knowledge base portal** > **Team auditing** in the Knowledge base portal.
  2. In the **Team auditing** tab, you'll find updates on visibility changes, title updates, and contributor modifications for each guide. For more information, read the article on [**Team auditing**](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/team-auditing).
</Expandable>
