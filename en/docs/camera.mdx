The **Camera** feature lets you record a video directly from your camera and overlay it on your explainer video or interactive video. You can also upload a pre-recorded video instead. Use this to add a personal touch, such as a face-cam narration or a presenter view, to your demo.

---

## Add a camera recording

1. Click **Camera** in the editor.

   - The Recording pane appears with a list of previously recorded videos (if any).
   - If videos already exist, click **Add new**.
2. Enable **Hide other elements** to hide every other element added in the timestamp.
3. In the **Add camera recording** pane, choose one of the following:

   | Option | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Record new** | Record a video directly using your camera |
   | **Select video** | Upload an already recorded video from your library |
4. In the **Time** field, enter the timestamp where the recording should appear. Use the **Fine-tune milliseconds** option for precise placement.
5. Select the desired **camera** and **microphone** from the dropdowns.
6. Click **Start recording**.<br /> A 3-second countdown begins before recording starts.<br /> <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793777537419-2z1zugmbynm-Camera--20record.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Add camera recording pane" />
7. During recording:

   - Click **Pause** and **Resume** as needed.
   - Click the **Retake** icon to start over.
   - Click **Discard** to delete the current recording.
   - Click **Stop** to end the recording.
8. After stopping, click **Save and continue**. The system processes and adds the video to the demo.
9. If needed, click **Record again** to capture another recording.

---

## Configuring the camera recording

Once saved, the recorded video overlays on the interactive video. Use the right panel to configure it:

1. Enable the **Hide other elements** toggle for a distraction free editing.
2. In the Time field, enter the time at which the recorded video is placed.
3. Click **Replace video** to swap the current recording with another.
4. Click the **Play** icon to preview the recording.
5. Click **Record new** to capture a fresh video.

<strong>Shape:</strong><br /> Choose a display shape for the video overlay.

- **Circle**
- **Square**
- **Wide**
- **Portrait**.

<strong>Position:</strong><br /> Select where the video appears on the demo screen.

<strong>Subtitles:</strong>

Turn on the **Subtitles** toggle to auto-generate subtitles if speech is detected in the recording.

Click **Apply** to save all changes.<br /> <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793777541324-hq4az96geem-Edit-20camera-20recording.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit camera recording pane" />

## Managing recorded videos

All recorded videos are listed in the **Camera recordings** pane.

- Click any recorded videos to modify it.
- To delete a recording, hover over it, click the **More** icon, and select **Delete**.<br /> <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793777541586-zlhhau6tlmj-Manage-20camera-20recording.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Camera recordings interface showing options to add new recordings and delete existing ones." />

 Note

You cannot create two recordings at the same timestamp.

## Best practices

- <strong>Use a stable camera angle.</strong> Ensure your camera is steady and well-lit before starting the recording.
- <strong>Test your microphone first.</strong> Check that the correct microphone is selected to avoid silent or low-quality audio.
- <strong>Use the Fine-tune option for accuracy.</strong> When placing a recording at a specific moment, use millisecond precision to sync it with the demo action.
- <strong>Enable subtitles for clarity.</strong> If your recording contains speech, turn on subtitles to improve accessibility.
- <strong>Preview before applying.</strong> Always use the Play icon to review the recording in context before clicking Apply.
