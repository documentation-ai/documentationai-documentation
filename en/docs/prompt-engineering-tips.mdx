In Document360, Eddy AI works best when you give it clear, focused instructions. This guide covers practical techniques for writing better prompts, whether you are creating content, asking questions, or building out a knowledge base for your team or customers.

---

### Writing Better Prompts

The following tips apply whenever you ask Eddy AI to generate or summarize content.

**1\. Be specific**

Vague instructions produce vague results. State exactly what you need, including length, tone, and focus. Example: "Generate a 100-word summary about the benefits of Document360's knowledge base."

**2\. Request step-by-step format for procedures**

When you need instructions or how-to content, tell Eddy AI to list steps and include where to click. Example: "List the steps to publish an article in Document360, including where to click and how to configure settings." instead of just saying "Explain how to publish an article."

**3\. Frame prompts for comparisons**

If you need a comparison, say explicitly so Eddy AI structures the output as a side-by-side or contrast. Example: "Compare the key differences between Document360's private and public knowledge base setups."

**4\. Target specific sections when summarizing**

For long articles, point Eddy AI to the exact section you want summarized rather than asking for a general summary. Example: "Summarize the benefits and limitations section of the article on Document360's multi-language support."

**5\. Add context for recommendations**

Include who the content is for and what problem it solves. Context helps Eddy AI give relevant suggestions. Example: "Recommend improvements to the onboarding process in a knowledge base article for new users."

**6\. Break complex requests into smaller tasks**

If your topic is technical or wide-ranging, split it into multiple focused prompts. This keeps outputs precise and usable.

### Tips for Readers Using Eddy AI

These tips are for people searching or asking questions in the knowledge base through Eddy AI.

1. **Converse naturally**

Talk to Eddy AI the way you would talk to a human. Full sentences work better than keyword searches.

- Instead of: <em>"Search for articles on templates"</em>
- Try: <em>"Eddy AI, find me articles explaining Document360 templates."</em>

2. **Provide context**

Include background in your question. The more Eddy AI knows about your situation, the more relevant the answer.

- Instead of: <em>"Errors in the documentation"</em>
- Try: <em>"Eddy AI, help me identify and resolve errors in the Document360 user guide."</em>

3. **Assign a persona**

Ask Eddy AI to respond as a specific type of expert when that helps frame the answer.

- Instead of: <em>"Search for software updates"</em>
- Try: <em>"Eddy AI, imagine you're our IT expert and update me on the latest software changes."</em>

4. **Experiment freely**

Eddy AI responds well to creative or open-ended prompts. Try different angles if the first result is not quite right.

- Instead of: <em>"Knowledge base structure"</em>
- Try: <em>"Eddy AI, imagine you're a content strategist and suggest an ideal knowledge base structure."</em>

### Question Prompting Best Practices

These rules apply when asking questions directly in Eddy AI's search or chat interface.

**A. Ask complete questions, not just keywords**

Write full questions instead of entering keyword fragments. Complete questions give Eddy AI the intent and context needed to return precise answers.

- Instead of keywords: "Website hosting pricing plans"
- Try asking complete question: "What are the pricing plans for website hosting services?"

**B. Avoid broad or vague queries**

Narrow your question to a specific situation or need. Specific questions produce targeted answers; broad ones often return generic results.

- Instead of general question: "Health insurance"
- Try asking specific question: "What health insurance plans are available for full-time employees?"

**C. Use standard terminology**

Use commonly accepted terms rather than informal or internal shorthand. Standard terms help Eddy AI match your question with the right content.

- Instead of non-standard terminology: "Specs of your top-of-the-line tech device"
- Try using standard terminology: "What are the specifications of the latest model of the tech device?"

Use these prompts when building or maintaining content in your knowledge base.

**Categorize content** Ask Eddy AI to suggest categories for new articles to keep your knowledge base organized. <em>Example: "Suggest categories for an article that discusses &#123;&#123; glossary.API&#125;&#125; integration and customization in Document360."</em>

**Compile best practices** Ask Eddy AI to pull together best practices for a topic and present them as a structured list. <em>Example: "List best practices for writing comprehensive how-to articles for a knowledge base aimed at developers."</em>

**Create troubleshooting guides** For troubleshooting sections, give Eddy AI the problem context so it generates step-by-step solutions. <em>Example: "Create a troubleshooting guide for resolving common login issues in Document360."</em>

## Use Cases by Industry

Eddy AI is a versatile tool that can streamline content creation across various industries, whether you're building an LMS, maintaining a customer support knowledge base, or generating compliance documentation.

### Learning Management Systems (LMS)

**Course creation**

- <strong>Generate outline:</strong> Ask Eddy AI to quickly draft a structured course plan with topics and subtopics. For example, "Create an outline for a beginner's course on cloud computing."
- <strong>Segment content:</strong> Break long-form content into smaller modules. For example, "Split this article on cybersecurity fundamentals into 5 lessons, each with a title and brief summary."
- <strong>Create quiz questions:</strong> Generate assessments for each module. For example, "Generate quiz questions for each module in a cloud computing course."
- <strong>Create FAQs:</strong> Build clarification points for specific modules. For example, "Create FAQs for the module on cloud security."

**Learner engagement**

- <strong>Adaptive follow-up:</strong> Recommend content based on learner progress. For example, "Suggest advanced articles for learners who have completed the beginner's cloud computing course."
- <strong>Revision guide:</strong> Create quick summaries for knowledge retention. For example, "Generate a revision guide summarizing the key points of the data privacy module."

---

### Customer Support Knowledge Base

**Creating troubleshooting guides**

- Prompt Eddy AI with specific support scenarios. For example, "Generate a step-by-step troubleshooting guide for common login issues in an e-commerce platform."
- For error code documentation, try: "Create documentation explaining the causes and fixes for error code 403 in web hosting."

**Organizing FAQs for support agents**

- Generate FAQs from common customer interactions. For example, "Create a list of FAQs for common billing issues faced by customers on our platform."
- Keep FAQs current by updating them with new data. For example, "Update our current FAQ section with recent questions about product integration."

---

### Compliance Documentation

**Generating compliance checklists**

- Ask Eddy AI to build checklists based on industry standards. For example, "Generate a checklist of mandatory compliance requirements for healthcare data privacy regulations (HIPAA)."
- Keep checklists up to date with prompt like: "Update the checklist with the latest changes to GDPR regulations."

<strong>Creating Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)</strong>

- In regulated environments, SOPs need to be clear and detailed. For example, "Draft a Standard Operating Procedure for handling customer data in compliance with GDPR."

---

### Software and Tech Companies

**API documentation**

- Generate usage examples and integration instructions. For example, "Generate step-by-step instructions on how to integrate our API with third-party services."
- Keep docs current after releases. For example, "Update the API documentation to reflect the changes in the latest version."

**Release notes and feature documentation**

- Summarize new releases clearly and concisely. For example, "Generate release notes for version 4.2 of our software, highlighting the new features and bug fixes."
- Create guides for newly shipped features. For example, "Create a user guide for the new reporting dashboard feature in our SaaS platform."

---

### Retail and E-Commerce

**Product documentation**

- Generate product guides that cover setup, usage, and troubleshooting. For example, "Create a product guide for a smart home security system, covering setup, troubleshooting, and usage tips."
- Create comparison content to help customers decide. For example, "Compare the features of three smart home security systems available on our platform."

**Order and return procedures**

Simplify customer-facing processes with clear step-by-step content. For example, "Generate step-by-step instructions for returning a product purchased on our e-commerce site."

</Expandable>
