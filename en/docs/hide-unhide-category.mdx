---
title: Hide & Unhide category
description: Learn how to hide and unhide categories in your knowledge base, keeping content accessible for your team while managing visibility for readers.
---

Hiding a category removes it from the knowledge base site while keeping it fully accessible in the portal for your team. Hidden categories appear with a strikethrough in the **Categories & Articles** pane, making them easy to identify without affecting your published content.

 NOTE

Hiding a category is different from deleting it. Hidden categories remain in the portal and can be unhidden at any time. Deleted categories are moved to the recycle bin.

---

## When to hide a category

- The category is being drafted or reviewed and you do not want readers to see incomplete content.
- The content is seasonal or time-sensitive and only relevant at certain times.
- A product feature or release is not yet live and you want to keep the documentation hidden until it is.
- The content is not yet ready for a specific audience but your team needs to continue working on it internally.

---

## Hide a category

1. Navigate to **Documentation** () in the left navigation bar.
2. Hover over the category in the **Categories & Articles** pane and click the **More** () icon.
3. Select **Hide** ().

The category appears with a strikethrough in the **Categories & Articles** pane, indicating it is hidden from the knowledge base site.

You can also hide a category from the **More** () icon inside a **Folder** or **Index** category, by selecting multiple items inside a Folder or Index category and clicking **Hide** (), or by hovering over a specific article or subcategory and clicking its **More** () icon > **Hide**.

 CAUTION

When you hide a parent category, all its subcategories and articles are also hidden. Unhiding the parent restores visibility for all content within it.

---

## Unhide a category

1. Navigate to **Documentation** () in the left navigation bar.
2. Hover over the hidden category in the **Categories & Articles** pane and click the **More** () icon.
3. Select **Unhide** ().

The category is immediately visible on the knowledge base site again.

You can also unhide from the **More** () icon inside a Folder or Index category, or by selecting multiple hidden items and clicking **Unhide** ().

---

## What happens when you hide a category

- The category and all its subcategories and articles are removed from the knowledge base site.
- Hidden categories remain visible in the portal with a strikethrough, so your team can still access and edit them.
- Readers cannot access hidden content, not even via a direct link.
- When you unhide a category, it becomes visible on the knowledge base site immediately.

---

## Best practices

- Use Hide instead of Delete for temporary removal. If there is any chance the content will be needed again, hide it rather than delete it. There is no time limit on hidden categories.
- Check for linked articles before hiding. If published articles in other categories link to content inside a hidden category, those links will be broken for readers. Review inbound links before hiding high-traffic categories.
- Communicate with your team. When hiding a shared category, let other contributors know so they do not spend time looking for content that appears missing.
- Combine with workflow review. Use hidden categories alongside workflow status to manage in-progress content. Keep it hidden until it reaches a published state.

## FAQ

<Expandable title="What is the difference between hiding and deleting a category?">
  **Hide** removes the category from the knowledge base site but keeps it fully accessible in the portal for editing. It can be unhidden at any time with no impact on the content.

  **Delete** removes the category from both the portal and the site, placing it in the recycle bin for 30 days before permanent deletion.

  Use Hide for temporary removal. Use Delete when the content is no longer needed.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can readers access a hidden category via a direct link?">
  No. Hidden categories and their articles are not accessible to readers, not even via a direct URL. Readers will encounter a 404 error if they try to access hidden content directly.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="When I hide a parent category, are its subcategories and articles also hidden?">
  Yes. Hiding a parent category hides all its subcategories and articles. Unhiding the parent restores visibility for all content within it.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I hide a category after it has been published?">
  Yes. You can hide a category at any time, including after it has been published. The category is removed from the knowledge base site immediately but remains available in the portal.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I hide multiple subcategories at once?">
  Yes. Inside a **Folder** or **Index** category, select the checkboxes next to the items you want to hide and click the **Hide** () icon from the bulk action bar.
</Expandable>
