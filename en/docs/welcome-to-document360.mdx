Build a knowledge base

A self-service help center where customers find answers before they reach your support team.

[Set up your knowledge base →](https://document360.com/solutions/external-knowledge-base/)

Write API documentation

Interactive API references that developers can read, test, and integrate with, without leaving the docs.

[Get started with API docs →](https://document360.com/solutions/api-documentation/)

Create SOPs or internal guides

Turn scattered processes into structured guides your whole team can follow without second-guessing.

[Build internal documentation →](https://document360.com/solutions/standard-operating-procedures/)

Deploy an AI chatbot

Train a chatbot on your docs, websites, files, FAQs, and support tickets to answer reader questions instantly.

[Set up your AI chatbot →](https://document360.com/ai/chat-bot/)

Migrate from another platform

Already on another platform? Move your docs to Document360 and pick up right where you left off.

[Import your content →](https://document360.com/migration-guide/)
