The **AI title recommender** is an Eddy AI feature in Document360 that generates optimized article titles based on your content. Using Natural Language Processing (NLP), it analyzes your article, identifies the key information, and surfaces up to three title options — helping you write clearer, more discoverable documentation without having to brainstorm titles from scratch.

---

## When to use the AI title recommender

Use the AI title recommender when you want to:

- Name a brand-new article and need a starting point that reflects the content accurately.
- Rename an existing article whose title no longer matches what the content covers.
- Improve SEO discoverability by replacing vague titles with NLP-generated, keyword-aligned alternatives.
- Maintain title consistency across a large knowledge base with many contributors.

---

## Before you begin

Before using the AI title recommender, confirm the following:

- Your article's **pre-processed word count** exceeds **50 words**. Pre-processing excludes HTML tags, images, URLs/links, and code blocks from the count — so your visible word count in the editor may be higher than the pre-processed count.
- You have available **AI credits**. Each time you click **Suggest title**, one credit is consumed — regardless of whether you save or discard the suggestion.

 NOTE

To see which languages are supported by Eddy AI, refer to the [AI writer suite — multilingual support](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/ai-writer-suite#multilingual-support-for-eddy-ai-writer-suite) article.

## Use the AI title recommender

The steps differ slightly depending on which editor your article uses.

### AI title recommender in Markdown

1. Navigate to the desired article in the **Knowledge base portal**.
2. Click the article title at the top of the editor. A panel appears.
3. Click **Ask Eddy AI**. Eddy generates three title suggestions.

4. Select your preferred title using the radio button.
5. Click **Generate more suggestions** if none of the initial three titles fit — three additional suggestions are generated.
6. Click **Choose** to apply the selected title.
7. Use the **Like** () or **Dislike** () buttons to rate the suggestions. This feedback helps improve future recommendations.

### AI title recommender in Advanced WYSIWYG editor

1. Navigate to the desired article in the **Knowledge base portal**.
2. Click the article title inside the article content area. A toolbar appears.
3. Select **Suggest title**. Eddy AI generates three title suggestions.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793778264526-1txqabqxsg9-Screenshot-AI_title_recommender_advanced_wysiwyg.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Advanced WYSIWYG editor showing the Suggest title panel with three AI-generated title options" />

4. Select your preferred title using the radio button.
5. Click **Generate more suggestions** to see three more alternatives.
6. Click **Choose** to apply the title.
7. For published or draft articles, a **Title update confirmation prompt** appears. Click **Yes** to confirm the change.
8. Use the **Like** () or **Dislike** () buttons to share feedback on the suggestions.

### What counts as a pre-processed word?

Pre-processing filters out the following elements before counting words:

- HTML tags
- Images
- URLs and links
- Code blocks

For example, if your editor shows 220 words at the bottom, the actual pre-processed count may be lower. Ensure the pre-processed word count exceeds 50 for the recommender to work.

## Best practices

- <strong>Write the article body first.</strong> The more content Eddy has to analyze, the more accurate and relevant the title suggestions will be. Avoid running the recommender on stub articles.
- <strong>Use feedback buttons.</strong> Clicking () Like or () Dislike after each generation helps the model improve over time for your team.
- <strong>Generate more if needed.</strong> If the first three suggestions don't fit, click **Generate more suggestions** before dismissing the panel — each additional set counts as one credit.
- <strong>Treat suggestions as a starting point.</strong> You can edit the suggested title after applying it. Use the AI output as a foundation, then fine-tune for brand voice or specificity.
- <strong>Monitor credit usage.</strong> If your team generates titles frequently, check usage in **Settings > AI settings > Eddy AI settings** to avoid hitting the soft limit mid-month.
