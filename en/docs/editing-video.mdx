After recording a video in the **Editor**, the **Video editor** opens automatically so you can enhance it before inserting it into your article. You can also reopen the editor anytime by selecting the video in the article and clicking **Edit** (  ), or by clicking the video's title in [Document360 Engage](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/document360-engage) and clicking **Edit**.

---

## What you can do in the Video editor

Each tool is covered in detail in its own article, see the links below.

- [Call to action](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/call-to-action) \- Add buttons that link to URLs or display messages during playback
- [Blur](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/blur-md) \- Mask credentials, PII, or sensitive UI elements at specific timestamps
- [Spotlight](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/spotlight-1) \- Draw attention to key areas of the screen during playback
- [Highlight](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/highlight-1) \- Emphasize specific areas of the screen during playback
- [Crop](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/crop-1) \- Resize and reframe the video to fit your article layout
- [Trim](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/trim-1) \- Cut unwanted sections from the start, middle, or end
- [Image](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/image-1) \- Insert images into the video at specific timestamps
- [Text](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/text-1) \- Add text overlays to annotate or label parts of the recording
- [Camera](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/camera) \- Include a camera feed overlay within the explainer video
- [Music](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/music-1) \- Add background music to the video
- [Voice](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/voice-1) \- Add AI voiceover in multiple languages or enable auto-generated subtitles
- [Annotation](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/annotation) \- Add contextual notes, insights, announcements, or alerts at specific timestamps
- [Zoom](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/zoom) \- Magnify a selected area of the screen at specific timestamps to focus attention on fine detail

## Create the video in other languages

If your knowledge base serves readers in more than one language, you can create a version of the video for each language from the same recording. You don't need to record the video again.

The following elements can have different content in each language:

- Call to action
- Text
- Subtitles
- AI voiceover, which is generated in the language of the script

Visual elements such as blur, spotlight, highlight, crop, and trim stay the same across all language versions.

To create a language version, translate the video from Document360 Engage. For the steps, see [Translate content](https://docs.document360.com/help/docs/document360-engage#translate-content).

To refine a translated version, click the video's title in Document360 Engage, select the language from the **Language** dropdown in the preview, and click **Edit**. Then edit the call to action, text, or subtitles for that language. The AI voiceover is generated from the translated text in the same language.

Readers see the version that matches the language they select on the knowledge base site. If the video isn't translated into that language, readers see the default language version.

## Managing added elements

In the **Added elements** dropdown, you can view all elements applied to the video:

- Click **Edit** (  ) to modify an element
- Click **Delete** (  ) to remove it
- Click **Eye** (  ) to hide mouse clicks across the video

Click **Close** to save all changes.
