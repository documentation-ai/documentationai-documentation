title: Advanced elements
description: Transform your projects with advanced elements like annotations, media, and engagement tools for a polished, interactive experience.
---

Advanced elements are layered additions that you can place on any step in your project. They give you precise control over what viewers see, hear, and interact with, letting you transform a basic screen recording into a polished, guided experience.

Advanced elements are organized into four categories:

- **Annotation**: Add visual markers and text to explain or emphasize content on screen. Includes Blur, Spotlight, Text, Highlight, and Image.
- **Media**: Enrich your steps with audio and video layers. Includes Music, Voice, and Camera.
- **Editing**: Refine the visual boundaries of your steps. Includes Crop and Trim.
- **Engagement**: Prompt viewers to take action or explore further. Includes Hotspot and Call to Action.

Each element can be independently configured with its own position, timing, animation, and styling - giving you full creative control over how and when it appears on screen.
