Start here - Setup

## Turn on Eddy AI

Eddy AI features are grouped into three suites that you turn on independently: the **AI writer suite** for authors, the **AI search suite** for your knowledge base site, and the **AI premium suite** for glossary and duplicate content tools. You need the Admin or Owner role to change these settings.

1. In the Knowledge base portal, select **Settings** in the left navigation bar.
2. Go to **AI settings** > **Eddy AI settings**.
3. Expand the suite you want to configure: **AI writer suite**, **AI search suite**, or **AI premium suite**.
4. Turn on the toggle for each feature your team needs.

Before you scale

<a id="limits-and-limitations"></a>

## Considerations

Check these limits before you roll Eddy AI out to your team.

| Item | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Editor** | The AI writer, AI FAQ generator, and Summarize changes work only in the Advanced WYSIWYG editor. |
| **Languages** | The AI writer suite and AI search suite support English and 21 other languages. The AI FAQ generator and AI generation of AI & search descriptions support English only. |
| **Indexed content** | AI assistive search answers only from published content. Changes appear in answers within about 15 minutes of publishing. |
| **Access control** | AI assistive search never answers from articles a reader is not allowed to view. |
| **AI style guide** | The style guide shapes content Eddy AI generates. It does not check or block content written manually. |
| **AI chatbot** | One chatbot or widget can be active per website domain. Each chatbot holds up to 40 MB of source content by default. |
| **Usage** | Eddy AI features have monthly usage limits. You can turn on email alerts at 80%, 90%, and 100% usage. |

## Best practices

- <strong>Turn on only the suites your team uses.</strong> Writers get the most from the AI writer suite, and support teams from the AI search suite.
- <strong>Set up your AI style guide before you generate content at volume.</strong> Every draft then starts in your voice and structure.
- <strong>Review every AI-generated draft before you publish.</strong> Check product names, UI labels, and version details against the product.
- <strong>Write content that AI can retrieve accurately.</strong> Run new articles through the [Gen AI friendly content checklist](/en/docs/gen-ai-friendly-content-checklist), and use the [prompt template](/en/docs/prompt-template-gen-ai-friendly-writing) when you draft with AI.
- <strong>Review unanswered queries every week.</strong> They show you which articles to write next.

## FAQ

<Expandable title="Does Eddy AI use my content to train AI models?">
  No. Eddy AI sends content to OpenAI through its API, and OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. For details on data handling, security policies, and safety testing, read the [Eddy AI trust center](/en/docs/eddy-ai-trust-center).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Why can't I see the Eddy AI options in my editor?">
  The AI writer is turned off by default. Turn it on in **Settings** > **AI settings** > **Eddy AI settings** under the AI writer suite. The AI writer works only in the Advanced WYSIWYG editor. For more help, read [AI writer](/en/docs/ai-writer).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I give Eddy AI my company's name and voice?">
  Yes. You can rename the assistant, choose the tone and length of its answers, and replace its icon. Read [AI customization](/en/docs/ai-customization).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What is the difference between AI assistive search and the AI chatbot?">
  AI assistive search answers questions in your knowledge base site search, using your published articles. The AI chatbot is a separate assistant you deploy on any website and train on several sources, including web pages, files, FAQs, and support tickets. It can also hand users off to a support ticket.
</Expandable>
