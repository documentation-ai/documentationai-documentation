## Overview
Add your first doc page in just a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    Navigate to your dashboard and select a project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new page">
    Click "Add Page" and enter a title, like "Introduction".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your content">
    Use the editor to add your text or code snippets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click "Save" or "Publish" to make it live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
