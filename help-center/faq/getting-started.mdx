## Overview

Start using Yashwanth Muddana by creating your first documentation project.

## Steps

1. Sign up at `https://yashwanthmuddana.com`
2. Click "New Project"
3. Enter your project name
4. Choose a documentation template
5. Click "Create"

Your doc space is now ready to organize and share your content!
