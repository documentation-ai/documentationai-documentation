## Getting Started

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What is WP Travel Engine and who is it for?" default-open="false">

  WP Travel Engine is a WordPress plugin for building travel and tour booking websites. Travel agencies, tour operators, and anyone selling travel packages online can use it to create trips, accept bookings, manage itineraries, set prices and availability, and collect payments.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do I need coding skills to use WP Travel Engine?" default-open="false">

  No. Create trips, set prices, and manage bookings from the WordPress dashboard without writing code. Advanced customizations may require basic CSS or PHP, but the core features work out of the box.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Is WP Travel Engine compatible with my theme?" default-open="false">

  WP Travel Engine works with most WordPress themes. For the best results, use the Travel Monster theme, which is designed specifically for WP Travel Engine. The plugin is also compatible with Elementor. If you encounter a compatibility issue, check the troubleshooting guides.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Pricing and Plans

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What is the difference between the free and Pro versions?" default-open="false">

  The free plugin includes core trip creation, booking, and basic payment integration. WP Travel Engine Pro adds features such as partial payments, email customization, itinerary building, extra services, group discounts, and additional add-ons.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How many documentation sites are included in my plan?" default-open="false">

  Your plan tier determines how many sites you can manage. Check your account page or contact support for the specific limit included with your plan.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I get a refund if WP Travel Engine is not right for me?" default-open="false">

  Yes. Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. Contact support with your order details to start the refund process.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do you offer discounts for non-profits or educational institutions?" default-open="false">

  Contact the support team to ask about special pricing for non-profit and educational use cases.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Payments and Billing

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Which payment gateways does WP Travel Engine support?" default-open="false">

  WP Travel Engine supports Stripe, PayPal Standard, PayPal Express, Authorize.Net, Razorpay, PayU, PayFast, PayHere, Midtrans, Himalayan Bank, WooCommerce, and check payments. Configure payment gateways under `WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments`.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I set up partial payments so customers can pay a deposit?" default-open="false">

  Partial Payments is a Pro add-on. Install and activate it, then configure the deposit percentage and due date in your trip settings. Customers pay the deposit at checkout and the remaining balance later.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why is a customer's payment not showing in my bookings?" default-open="false">

  Check that the payment gateway is configured with valid API keys. Verify the payment status in your gateway dashboard as well. If the payment is successful but still does not appear, check for plugin conflicts.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Trips and Bookings

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I create my first trip?" default-open="false">

  Go to `WordPress dashboard > Trips > Add New`. Complete the General Info, Date and Price, Itinerary, and other trip tabs. Publish the trip when you are ready. See the setup guide for a step-by-step walkthrough.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I set different prices for adults and children?" default-open="false">

  Yes. In the Date and Price tab of your trip, add separate pricing categories for adults and children. Use the Labels feature to rename `Adult` and `Child` to fit your booking form.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I handle trips that sell out?" default-open="false">

  When all seats are booked, WP Travel Engine automatically marks the trip as Sold Out. Use the Waitlist add-on to let customers join a waiting list for sold-out trips.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I offer group discounts?" default-open="false">

  Yes. The Group Discount add-on, available with Pro, lets you set percentage or fixed discounts based on the number of travelers booked together.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Emails and Notifications

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I customize the booking confirmation email?" default-open="false">

  Use the Email Customizer add-on, available with Pro, to change the layout, colors, logo, and text of booking and payment emails. Go to `WP Travel Engine > Email Customizer` in your dashboard.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why are my booking emails not being sent?" default-open="false">

  Your website may not be configured to send emails reliably. Use an SMTP plugin such as WP Mail SMTP or an email delivery service such as SendGrid. Also check that the correct email addresses are configured under `WP Travel Engine > Settings > Emails`.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I send different emails for different trips?" default-open="false">

  Yes. The Per Trip Emails add-on, available with Pro, lets you create custom email templates for individual trips. Each trip can then have its own booking confirmation message.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="My trip page shows a 404 error. How do I fix it?" default-open="false">

  Go to `Settings > Permalinks` in WordPress and click **Save Changes** to flush your permalinks. If the error continues, check that the predefined pages are configured correctly under `WP Travel Engine > Settings`.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="The Date and Price tab is not loading in the trip editor. What should I do?" default-open="false">

  A plugin or theme conflict usually causes this issue. Deactivate other plugins one at a time to identify the conflict. Check that your PHP version also meets the minimum requirement.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why can I not expand the trip edit sections such as Overview and Itinerary in the backend?" default-open="false">

  Another plugin or your theme may be causing a JavaScript conflict. Clear your browser cache, disable other plugins, or switch to a default theme to identify the cause.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="I am getting 503 resource limit errors. What does that mean?" default-open="false">

  A 503 resource limit error means your hosting server has reached its memory or CPU limits. Contact your hosting provider about upgrading your plan, or reduce the number of active plugins.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Account and License

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I activate my Pro add-on license key?" default-open="false">

  Download the add-on from your WP Travel Engine account, then install and activate it on your site. Open the add-on settings page and enter your license key. See the license activation guide for detailed instructions.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I download add-ons and themes after purchasing?" default-open="false">

  Log in to your WP Travel Engine account at [wptravelengine.com](https://wptravelengine.com), open **Downloads**, and download your purchased add-ons and themes.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I upgrade my plan?" default-open="false">

  Log in to your account and open the upgrade page. You only pay the difference between your current plan and the new plan. Contact support if you need help.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I cancel my subscription?" default-open="false">

  Log in to your WP Travel Engine account, open **Subscriptions**, and cancel your subscription. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
