---
title: Documentation Page Not Loading
description: How to fix if a documentation page won’t load.
---

## Symptoms
- Docs page shows blank or error
- Page takes too long to respond

## Possible Issues
- Browser cache problems
- No internet connection

## Quick Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh your browser">
    Press `Ctrl+F5` or clear cache.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your connection">
    Make sure you are online and try reloading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try another browser">
    Use Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.
  </Step>
</Steps>
