---

The MkDocs Developer Guide provides documentation for developers of third party themes and plugins. Please see the [Contributing Guide](/about/contributing) for information on contributing to MkDocs itself. You can jump directly to a page listed below, or use the *next* and *previous* buttons in the navigation bar at the top of the page to move through the documentation in order.
