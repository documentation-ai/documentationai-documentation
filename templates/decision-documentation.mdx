## Use this template to document significant decisions

A decision record captures the context, options, and reasoning behind a significant choice so your team can understand why it was made, even months later. Use this template for every decision that shapes architecture, process, or product direction. Clear decision records reduce repeated debates and make future changes easier to evaluate.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">

Use this template for every significant decision. Fill in the Decision Details table first, then work through each section. Update the Status field as the decision progresses from proposed to accepted to implemented.

</Callout>
