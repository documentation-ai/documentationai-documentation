Localized titles let you show different titles for different languages on your published site.

Visitors see the title that matches the language they’re browsing in. If a translation isn’t set, GitBook shows the fallback title instead.

Use `localizedTitle` for header links and buttons, footer links, and footer groups. See [Layout and structure](/docs/manage-your-site/customization/layout-and-structure).

<Callout kind="info">
  Localized titles only appear when your site includes content in more than one language.
</Callout>

### Set up localized titles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the structure editor" titleType="h3">
    From your docs site, open **Site structure** in the site sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the title you want to translate" titleType="h3">
    Open the title field for your site, a section, or a section group.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the fallback title" titleType="h3">
    By default, the fallback title is set to the current section name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add each translated title" titleType="h3">
    Open the language selector again and choose a language.

    Enter the title for that language, then repeat for each language you support.
  </Step>
</Steps>
