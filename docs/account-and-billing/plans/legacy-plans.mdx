We’ve [updated our pricing model](https://www.gitbook.com/blog/new-site-sections#new-pricing-for-new-users) recently, to accommodate for many of the new features we’ve been releasing for published sites. Because of this, there is a chance you may still be on our old pricing model.

Some of the new features we are releasing are only available on our updated pricing plans. Please [contact support](mailto:support@gitbook.com) if you would like to learn more and upgrade your pricing.

See our [current plans](/docs/account-and-billing/plans).

### AI pricing

GitBook's AI features now run on AI credits. Here's what changed:

- <strong>Premium is now Essential:</strong> The Premium site plan has been renamed Essential. Your features stay the same.
- <strong>AI credits:</strong> Each Essential site includes 2,000 AI credits per month, and each Ultimate site includes 10,000. You can add a monthly credit pack if you need more.
- <strong>Translations add-on:</strong> The Translations add-on is only available to organizations on legacy pricing. On current plans, auto-updating translations use AI credits.

### Updated pricing

We've made some changes to our plans to simplify publishing and better align features with site plans. Here's a summary:

- **Pro Plan**: No longer includes a monthly platform fee. You can purchase and publish sites without restrictions, even on the Free plan.
- **Organization Plan**: Now focused exclusively on collaboration.

AI Assistant Ask is available on Essential and Ultimate site plans. AI Assistant Chat and other advanced AI features are available on Ultimate site plans.
