### Allow readers to export a PDF version of your published content

To enable or disable PDF export for visitors to your [published docs site](/docs/publish/publish-a-docs-site), open **Customize** in the site sidebar and navigate to **Configure → Page actions**. From there, you can toggle **Export as PDF** on or off.

This setting determines whether or not **readers of your published content can download it in PDF format**. This feature is only available for **Essential and Ultimate sites**.

### Export your own internal content as PDF

However you decide to configure your published docs sites, all logged-in members of an organization on a Pro or Enterprise plan can export a page, or an entire section, from your internal knowledge base as a PDF file.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Note that links across sections are not currently supported when exporting internal content to PDF.
</Callout>

#### Export an individual page

1. Open the page you want to export, then open the page’s [Actions menu](/docs/reference/gitbook-ui#the-actions-menu)

   <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793686610515-ggy419gxrp-spaces-2FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X-2Fuploads-2F89MTSo5XRpPMVr1T0rxS-2Factions.svg?rasterize-bypass=true" alt="The Actions menu icon in GitBook" />

   next to the page title.
2. Select **Export to PDF > Current page**.
3. Wait for the page to load, then click the **Print or save as PDF** button in the upper right to open your browser’s Print menu.
4. From here, you can save the page as a PDF or open it in your PDF viewer using the typical process for your browser.

#### Export an entire section

1. Open the [Actions menu](/docs/reference/gitbook-ui#the-actions-menu)

   <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793686610515-ggy419gxrp-spaces-2FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X-2Fuploads-2F89MTSo5XRpPMVr1T0rxS-2Factions.svg?rasterize-bypass=true" alt="The Actions menu icon in GitBook" />

   next to the page title and choose **Export as PDF > All pages**. Alternatively, open the section’s **Actions menu**

   <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-305180ff-8bbf-40a5-bdbf-7db82ff17465/doc-37e0ac78-8ede-48d7-8b5d-fcbc080a78a6/1793686680250-b01qn5scaep-spaces-2FNkEGS7hzeqa35sMXQZ4X-2Fuploads-2FPnnI41SqLSaKBNwT98fW-2Factions-horizontal.svg?rasterize-bypass=true" alt="The Actions menu icon in GitBook" />

   in the [section header](/docs/reference/gitbook-ui#space-header) and choose **Export as PDF** in the drop-down menu.<br /><br /><em>Note: This action is not available within a change request.</em>
2. Wait for the page to load, then click the **Print or save as PDF** button in the upper right to open your browser’s Print menu.
3. From here, you can save the page as a PDF or open it in your PDF viewer using the typical process for your browser.
